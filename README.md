## ⚡ About Me

I'm a Principal Engineer and Full-Stack Systems Architect based in Queensland, Australia, with over a decade of experience building and scaling software systems. My work centers on technical leadership, cloud architecture, infrastructure optimisation, and CI/CD automation—combining deep engineering execution with product pragmatism.

I specialise in modular system design, maintainability, and developer experience (DX), routinely applying AI-assisted development workflows to boost engineering speed and code quality. Whether leading cross-functional teams, mentoring engineers, or modernising complex codebases, I focus on building sustainable, high-performing software that solves real business problems.
